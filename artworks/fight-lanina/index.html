Fight La Niña

迎战拉尼娜

灯光装置 | 秦皇岛阿那亚 · 阿那亚戏剧节候鸟 300 | 2024

一座为戏剧节降温的灯光装置——在酷暑里，让凉意先拉开序幕。

极端气候现象“拉尼娜”降临在我们身边，带来持续长久的炎热，为阿那亚戏剧节候鸟300制造出全面新颖的冷却解决方案及具体措施，希望通过装置化的体现，为阿那亚戏剧节序幕拉开时的每一寸角落散布凉音，每一分烈阳热力澎湃的同时也提供冷却的能量。

A lighting installation that cools a theatre festival: amid the heat, coolness raises the curtain first.

The extreme climate phenomenon known as La Niña has arrived, bringing prolonged and intense heat. In response, a comprehensive and innovative cooling solution has been created specifically for Migratory Birds 300 at the Aranya Theatre Festival. Through an installation-based approach, the design aims to spread refreshing sounds throughout every corner as the festival’s opening unfolds, offering cooling energy amid the blazing sun and intense heat.
